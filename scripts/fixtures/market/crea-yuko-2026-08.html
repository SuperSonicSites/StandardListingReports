The number of homes sold through the MLS® System of the Yukon Real Estate Association totaled 56 units in August 2026. This was an increase of 19.1% (nine sales) from August 2025.

Home sales were 7.7% above the five-year average and 15% above the 10-year average for the month of August.

On a year-to-date basis, home sales totaled 365 units over the first eight months of the year. This was a decline of 11.8% from the same period in 2025.

“August delivered another encouraging month for our local markets. While sales eased from July totals, activity remained well ahead of the same period last year. The same goes for the number of new listings during the month. Overall inventory also extended its steady climb, marking seven consecutive months of month-over-month increases, remaining above historical norms for this time of year,” said Marc Perreault, President of the Yukon Real Estate Association. “Prices continued to show resilience, with the average resale selling price holding above $600,000 for a third straight month, edging higher than a year ago. Market conditions remained slightly in favour of sellers, but the balance between buyers and sellers continues to be relatively close. As we move into a new season, market dynamics can shift quickly from neighbourhood to neighbourhood. Working with a REALTOR® gives buyers and sellers access to local market expertise, helping them understand changing conditions, identify opportunities, and make confident, informed decisions based on real-time insights from their community.”

The average price of homes sold in August 2026 was $640,416, increasing by 8.4% from August 2025.

The more comprehensive year-to-date average price was $622,389, a gain of 8.1% from the first eight months of 2025.

The dollar value of all home sales in August 2026 was $35.9 million, up by 29.2% from the same month in 2025. This was also a new record for the month of August.

The number of new listings saw a substantial increase of 37.5% from August 2025. There were 88 new residential listings in August 2026. This was also the largest number of new listings added in the month of August in history.

New listings were 26.4% above the five-year average and 40.8% above the 10-year average for the month of August.

Active residential listings numbered 215 units on the market at the end of August, a small gain of 3.9% (eight listings) from the end of August 2025.

Active listings were 2.8% above the five-year average and 17.3% above the 10-year average for the month of August.

Months of inventory numbered 3.8 at the end of August 2026, down from the 4.4 months recorded at the end of August 2025 and on par with the long-run average of 3.8 months for this time of year. The number of months of inventory is the number of months it would take to sell current inventories at the current rate of sales activity.
